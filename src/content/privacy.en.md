## What statistics are collected

Statistics are counted by [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com) — open-source software installed on the site's own server. No data is passed to third-party services or ad networks.

When a page is viewed, the following is recorded:

- the page address and title;
- where the visitor came from — for example, hh.ru or a `?ref=` tag in the link;
- browser, operating system and screen width;
- country — derived from the IP address at the time of the request; the address itself is not stored;
- actions on a project page: the opened tab, document, screenshot viewing, following an external link.

## What is not collected

- cookies or other browser identifiers — your theme choice is stored only in your browser and is never sent anywhere;
- IP addresses — neither the statistics nor the site's web server record them;
- names, emails and phone numbers — the site has no forms.

So that a repeat view is not counted as a new visit, GoatCounter keeps a "browser + IP address → random visit number" mapping in memory for up to 8 hours. It is never written to disk or the database, and the random number cannot be traced back to anyone.

## If you prefer not to be counted

Block `stats.sumchenko-tech.ru` in your browser or ad blocker — the site keeps working fully.
